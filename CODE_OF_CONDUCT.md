# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Achilles to be a rigorous, welcoming project where people can disagree about
architecture, safety and evidence without making participation hostile. Contributors,
maintainers and community members are expected to treat one another with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific and curious.
- Critique ideas, claims and code—not people.
- State uncertainty and distinguish measurements from assumptions.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licences and responsible disclosure.
- Make room for newcomers while maintaining the project's engineering standards.
- Accept correction gracefully and help repair harm when it occurs.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, sustained disruption, or publishing another person's private information without
permission are not tolerated. Neither is using project infrastructure to facilitate abuse.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact options on the
maintainer's GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities must use the process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid conflicts of
interest. Good-faith technical disagreement is not a conduct violation.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, issue trackers, review channels and
public spaces where someone is representing Achilles.

This policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/)
and the [Django Code of Conduct](https://www.djangoproject.com/conduct/).
